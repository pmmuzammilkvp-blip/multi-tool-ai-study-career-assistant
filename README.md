# 🎓 Endura: AI Study & Career Assistant

Endura is an AI app for students. You can **learn from your own notes**, **practise quizzes**, and **get ready for interviews**, all in one place. You can type or **speak in Tamil or English**.

🔗 **Live demo:** (https://endura-mentor.streamlit.app/)

> The demo runs on a free plan. If nobody used it for a while, the first load can take a minute or two. Upload a file in the Study Assistant first, because the app starts with no documents.

## ✨ Features

### 📚 Study Assistant
Upload your PDF, PowerPoint, Word or text files and ask questions about them. An AI agent decides by itself whether to look in your files, search the internet, or just answer. It shows a 🔧 badge so you can see which one it used. It remembers the chat, so you can ask follow-up questions like "tell me more about that".

### 🎙️ Voice Mode
Talk to the AI instead of typing. Speak your question in Tamil or English and the AI answers in text and in voice.

### 📝 Quiz Generator
Creates multiple-choice questions from your uploaded notes. You get instant feedback, a final score, and a review of the questions you got wrong. It also finds your weak topics and can make a new quiz just for them.

### 🎧 Audio Explainer
Type or speak any topic and get a short spoken explanation in Tamil or English.

### 🧠 Mind Map Generator
Turns any topic into a mind map with branches. You can download it as an image or a web page.

### 🎞️ PPT Generator
Type a topic and get a ready-to-present PowerPoint slide deck that you can download.

### 📄 Resume Analyzer
Upload your resume and paste a job description. It gives a match score, the skills you have, the skills you are missing, and tips to improve.

### 🗣️ Mock Interview
A practice interview made from your resume, with up to three rounds:
- **Technical round**: questions about your skills and projects
- **Coding round**: a coding problem. Write your solution and explain your approach
- **HR round**: questions about teamwork, challenges and goals

You can answer by typing or by speaking. Every answer gets a score out of 10, feedback, and a better sample answer. At the end you see a score for each round and a total score.

### 🐛 AI Code Debugger
Paste code in any programming language. It explains the bug and gives the fixed code in the same language. For Python, you can also run the fixed code to check it.

### 💻 Code Bot
A chat for coding help. Ask it to write, explain or fix code. It remembers the conversation and answers in the language you are using.

### 🧭 Agent Activity
Shows how many times the AI used document search, web search, or a direct answer, and a list of recent questions.

### 📊 Progress Dashboard
Shows your quiz scores and interview scores over time, your weak topics, and your full history.

### 🎤 Microphone on every tool
Every tool has a mic button, so you can speak your question instead of typing it.

## ⚠️ Good to know
- The app uses free AI plans. If many people use it at once, you may see "usage limit reached". Wait a minute and try again.
- Documents you upload are stored on the server and shared with other users of the same demo. Use files that are not private.
- Web answers can sometimes be wrong or out of date, so check the sources shown.
- The mock interview coding round is reviewed by AI. The code is not run.
- The "Run code" button in the Code Debugger is turned off in the public demo for safety.
